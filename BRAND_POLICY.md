permission from the applicable rights holder.

Commercial use of the Hypha software does not grant a right to brand a product, service, fork, or
company as Hypha.

## 2. Uses allowed without prior written permission

You may:

- refer to the Hypha project by name in a truthful, non-misleading manner;
- state that a product or project is "built with Hypha", "powered by Hypha", "compatible with Hypha",
  or "based on Hypha", when that statement is accurate;
- reproduce the official Hypha logo, **without visual modification**, solely to identify, reference,
  attribute, or link to the official Hypha project;
- proportionally resize the official logo or convert its file format when doing so does not alter its
  colors, geometry, typography, relative spacing, composition, or visual appearance;
- use screenshots of official Hypha materials for commentary, documentation, research, reporting, or
  educational discussion, subject to applicable law.

These permissions do not allow the Hypha Brand Assets to become the primary branding of a
third-party product or service.

## 3. Uses that require prior written permission

Unless the applicable rights holder gives prior written permission, you may not:

- recolor, crop, stretch, skew, rotate, mirror, redraw, trace, animate, stylize, or otherwise alter the
  Hypha logo or wordmark;
- change the logo's typography, proportions, spacing, node/line geometry, color relationships, or
  composition;
- add shadows, gradients, outlines, badges, slogans, effects, decorative elements, or other graphics
  to the logo;
- remove, separate, rearrange, or substitute elements of the official logo;
- combine the Hypha logo with another logo, symbol, company name, product name, or mark to create a
  new or composite mark;
- create or distribute a confusingly similar or derivative Hypha logo, icon, mascot, wordmark, or
  visual identity;
- use Hypha or a confusingly similar name or mark as the name, logo, app icon, favicon, domain name,
  social-media handle, package branding, product branding, company branding, or service branding of
  a third party;
- use the Brand Assets on merchandise, advertising, paid promotions, tokens, collectibles, or other
  commercial branding materials except for truthful referential use expressly allowed above;
- use the Brand Assets in a way that suggests endorsement, certification, sponsorship, partnership,
  official status, or affiliation with the Hypha project when none exists;
- present a modified fork or third-party distribution as an official Hypha release.

## 4. Forks, modifications, and commercial products

You may modify, redistribute, and commercially use the Hypha **software** under Apache-2.0.

If you distribute a modified fork or build a commercial product from Hypha:

1. use your own primary product name and visual identity;
2. do not present the modified product as an official Hypha build or release;
3. do not use the Hypha logo as your product logo, app icon, or dominant commercial branding;
4. you may use accurate text such as "based on Hypha" or "powered by Hypha";
5. if you display the Hypha logo for attribution, keep it unmodified and visually subordinate to your
   own branding.

Permission to use the software or to make a permitted referential use of the Brand Assets does not
